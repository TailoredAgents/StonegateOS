## Executive verdict — baseline and current disposition

The original audit found a real but narrow invited-user MVP. That baseline description is retained in the finding text below so the remediation trail remains reviewable. The current working tree is no longer that five-page MVP: it now contains an account-rooted Partner Portal V2 with limited self-service onboarding, memberships/capabilities, MFA and session controls, a dedicated responsive shell, account locations, resumable booking drafts, policy-backed arrival windows and holds, job workspaces, explicit private media/proof, messages/notifications, commercial reads and payments, repeat/recurring/bulk tools, and staff application/team administration.

The five original product blockers now have material implementations:

1. **Availability:** one policy/capacity service now produces opaque two-hour partner windows from internal 30-minute candidates and reserves a feasible candidate through a ten-minute hold.
2. **Scheduling integrity:** portal, public quote, lead, and staff writers use the shared schedule lock/conflict foundation; exhaustive production-equivalent all-channel race evidence is still required.
3. **Photos and proof:** draft/job uploads, before/after requirements, job galleries, completion PDF/ZIP packages, and revocable share links now exist; live storage, interruption, malware, retention, and compare/dispute evidence remain incomplete.
4. **Account creation:** a verified-email application flow grants immediate limited access and supports an audited staff review/approval queue; the local applicant-to-approved-account browser transition passes, while production email and review canaries remain release gates.
5. **Job hub:** overview, jobs, timeline/actions, account approvals, locations, proof, billing/documents, reports, help, messages, templates, recurring work, and CSV intake now exist; several advanced commercial and operational workflows remain partial.

The original data-integrity defects have also been addressed in architecture and focused tests: V2 rescheduling operates through a replacement hold and preserves the account-owned job, job/media ownership is explicit, and portal DTOs do not serialize raw appointment IDs or internal planned starts. These changes are not promoted to fully closed until their row-specific browser, concurrency, downstream reconciliation, and canary evidence exists.

The complete ledger contains **99 findings: 12 P0, 53 P1, 29 P2, and 5 P3**. Each finding includes the required improvement and its evidence.

The original audit records the required additions and corrections; the implementation completion ledger at the end is the live closure record. No row is silently waived, and no row is marked **Closed** without its required manual/provider/canary evidence.

## Required target capability checklist

This is a product-completeness checklist, not an implementation sequence.

### Acquire and onboard

- Separate **Sign in** and **Request/Create partner account** journeys.
- Verify person and organization; collect persona, service area, needs, terms/privacy consent, and approval status.
- Support invite/approval, first-login checklist, profile/company setup, colleague invitations, roles, and organization switching.
- Retain protected return URLs and provide clear recovery/support at every auth state.

### Schedule confidently

- Query one canonical availability engine using policy, holds, all booking channels, statuses, buffers, service duration, crews/trucks/skills, geography, blackout/holiday rules, and partner SLA.
- Display only valid slots with timezone, arrival-window meaning, price state, and constraint explanations.
- Hold the chosen slot while the partner reviews/submits; recover with alternatives if it expires.
- Preserve one transaction/locking contract across partner, public, and staff channels.

### Describe the work completely

- Capture property/unit, onsite contacts, access, scope/items/materials/quantities, hazards/heavy items, equipment, deadline, PO/cost center, billing reference, completion requirements, notes, and alternate options.
- Support service plus add-ons, contracted/estimated/quote-required pricing, recurring/templates/bulk jobs, and persona-aware fields.
- Provide an accessible review/terms/policy step and durable draft recovery.

### Use photos and proof

- Upload from camera/files with previews, captions/types, progress, retry, limits, and private tenant-scoped storage.
- Request before, after, or full proof; show staff requirements and completion state.
- Deliver a before/after gallery and downloadable/shareable proof report with issue handling and retention rules.

### Manage the job

- Provide a detail workspace with full submitted request, reference, status timeline, changes, contacts, media/proof, price/invoice/payment/documents, notifications, and support/messages.
- Permit safe, policy-aware edits/change requests, reschedule, cancel, duplicate/rebook, and dispute/escalation.
- Separate actionable upcoming work from searchable/paginated history and exports.

### Manage the account

- Provide properties/locations/units with validation, serviceability, aliases, contacts, sensitive access controls, edit/archive, search/filter/import.
- Provide profile, organization, users/roles/approvals, notifications, billing, security/sessions, contracts/rates, documents, support, and reporting.
- Keep canonical appointment, calendar/dispatch, pricing, revenue/payment, media, and audit data consistent.

## Verified strengths to preserve

- Real PostgreSQL persistence; property creation and booking workflows are transactional.
- Strong current organization scoping on property use, booking reads, cancellation, and rescheduling; no direct cross-organization IDOR was found in audited routes.
- One-time magic links use random raw tokens with only hashes stored, are atomically consumed, and invalidate prior unused links.
- Public request-link and password-login endpoints are bounded, rate-limited, and non-enumerating at the API layer; token exchange is bounded and single-use but has the separate throttling gap recorded above.
- Sessions are random, hashed, revocable, server-expiring, and revalidate active user plus active/non-deleted organization state on every request.
- Production cookies are HTTP-only, Secure, and SameSite Lax.
- Admin deactivation revokes sessions and outstanding tokens transactionally.
- Booking creation/reschedule/cancel have meaningful idempotency, replay receipts, optimistic versioning, audit records, and transactional notification/calendar outbox work.
- A failed replacement leaves the original appointment intact during rescheduling.
- Negotiated price is looked up server-side; callers cannot submit an arbitrary amount.
- Notes are bounded and reach internal appointment notes plus confirmation/staff messaging.
- Existing appointment media storage is private, purpose-aware, caption-capable, soft-deletable, and suitable as a foundation once partner authorization is designed.
- The current UI has coherent cards, labels, headings, visible focus styling, useful empty-state CTAs, no tested horizontal overflow, and pending protection on booking/cancel mutations.
- In the 14-state local run, no page exception, console error, or definite Axe violation occurred.

## Baseline audit conclusion retained

The correct re-angle is not a visual reskin of the current five pages. The portal needs to become an account-aware scheduling and job workspace built on one canonical availability/conflict contract and one canonical job record. Live availability, protected photo/proof workflows, self-service onboarding, complete structured intake, and job detail/history are core architecture—not optional enhancements.

The existing authentication, tenancy checks, transactional booking mutations, idempotency, private media foundation, notifications, and basic responsive UI are worth preserving. The next planning pass should turn the P0/P1 findings into an implementation sequence with schema/API migration boundaries, UX flows, rollout/compatibility steps, and blocking acceptance tests.

The current disposition is governed by the completion ledger below: much of that re-angle now exists in the repository, while the remaining partial scope and release evidence keep the product from general availability.

### Explicit GA blockers

The portal is **not GA-ready** on this evidence. The following are blocking release even where substantial implementation exists:

1. **Every P0 remains unclosed.** `PORTAL-SCH-001`, `PORTAL-BOOK-003`, `PORTAL-BOOK-012`, and `PORTAL-ACC-001` now have current local browser/integration proof, while `PORTAL-MEDIA-002` and `PORTAL-MEDIA-005` have focused automated proof; all still lack their applicable production/provider/canary evidence. `PORTAL-SCH-002`, `PORTAL-SCH-004`, `PORTAL-SCH-005`, `PORTAL-BOOK-001`, `PORTAL-BOOK-004`, and `PORTAL-MEDIA-001` remain materially partial.
2. **Scheduling truth is not yet audit-wide proven.** Partner-specific/minimum-notice policy, all-channel status semantics, adversarial concurrency, and named resource/route constraints remain incomplete (`PORTAL-SCH-002`, `PORTAL-SCH-004`, `PORTAL-SCH-005`, `PORTAL-SCH-009`, `PORTAL-TEST-002`).
3. **The full commercial booking contract is incomplete.** Core intake, canonical base-plus-add-on pricing, review-only fallback, rescheduling, cancellation, and approval confirmation now have local browser evidence. The remaining blockers are deeper conditional equipment/deadline intake, complete dispatch/final-finance reconciliation, entitlement/effective-date variants, change-order and approval-rule administration, multi-stop work, and production/provider proof (`PORTAL-BOOK-001`, `PORTAL-BOOK-004`, `PORTAL-BOOK-005`, `PORTAL-BOOK-007`, `PORTAL-BOOK-008`, `PORTAL-BOOK-015` through `PORTAL-BOOK-019`, `PORTAL-BOOK-023`, `PORTAL-TEST-004`).
4. **Onboarding and account security lack release proof and residual settings depth.** Self-service access, staff approval, return paths, authoritative logout, passwords, memberships/invitations, MFA, and session lifetimes are implemented, but real-provider delivery, browser expiry/revocation/logout recovery, verified contact changes, organization/billing-contact editing, durable checklist state, persona tailoring, audit retention, physical-authenticator use, and role-matrix journeys remain (`PORTAL-ACC-001`, `PORTAL-ACC-005`, `PORTAL-ACC-010` through `PORTAL-ACC-013`, `PORTAL-ACC-016` through `PORTAL-ACC-018`, `PORTAL-TEST-001`).
5. **Media needs real-environment validation and remaining UX.** Real byte progress, stable retry/previews, private associations, completion packages, share controls, and an accessible compare foundation exist. Mobile interruption, client compression/atomic replace, dispute handling, malware-provider validation, live download/storage behavior, and approved retention policy are not complete or not proven (`PORTAL-MEDIA-001`, `PORTAL-MEDIA-003`, `PORTAL-MEDIA-004`, `PORTAL-TEST-003`).
6. **Release evidence remains incomplete beyond the now-passing local lanes.** Controlled failure depth, manual assistive-technology/zoom and cross-browser review, production p75 analytics, real provider/storage/payment smoke tests, a hosted CI result, and canary deployment remain absent (`PORTAL-UX-005`, `PORTAL-UX-011`, `PORTAL-UX-015`, `PORTAL-OPS-005`, `PORTAL-OPS-006`, `PORTAL-OPS-008`, `PORTAL-TEST-001`, `PORTAL-TEST-005`, `PORTAL-TEST-006`, `PORTAL-TEST-008`).

No row should be promoted to **Closed** solely because its implementation or focused tests pass. Closure requires the row-specific remaining evidence, plus successful rollout/canary validation where the feature touches production scheduling, authentication, storage, messaging, documents, payments, or tenant data.
