## Executive verdict — baseline and current disposition

The original audit found a real but narrow invited-user MVP. That baseline description is retained in the finding text below so the remediation trail remains reviewable. The current working tree is no longer that five-page MVP: it now contains an account-rooted Partner Portal V2 with verification-first applicant onboarding, memberships/capabilities, MFA and session controls, a dedicated responsive shell, verified account-owned location portfolios with recoverable merge, resumable booking drafts, global-plus-account scheduling policy, named resource assignment, arrival windows and holds, durable scheduling assistance plus cancellation/job-change review, explainable job actions, a safe ETA/team/issue job hub, explicit private media/proof, messages/notifications, canonical Quote V2, service agreements, exact quote-bound change orders and approval administration, payments and billing-dispute intake, repeat/recurring/bulk tools, persona presentation, personal profile editing, operational health reporting, and staff administration queues.

The five original product blockers now have material implementations:

1. **Availability:** one policy/capacity service now produces opaque two-hour partner windows from internal 30-minute candidates, reserves a feasible candidate through a ten-minute hold, and applies account policy only as a narrowing layer over Stonegate hours, notice, horizon, and instant-confirmation eligibility.
2. **Scheduling integrity:** portal, public quote, lead, and staff writers use the shared schedule lock/conflict foundation; exhaustive production-equivalent all-channel race evidence is still required.
3. **Photos and proof:** draft/job uploads, before/after requirements, job galleries, completion PDF/ZIP packages, and revocable share links now exist; live mobile/real-provider interruption, malware, retention, and compare/dispute evidence remain incomplete.
4. **Account creation:** the current flow verifies email into an applicant-only session and persists only an application before approval. It does not create a partner account, identity, membership, CRM contact, or portal authority until an authorized approval transaction. Approval creates an invited membership and queues purpose-bound activation; password setup or confirmation follows. A current applicant-to-activation browser/provider journey remains a release gate.
5. **Job hub:** overview, jobs, explainable action availability, durable change/cancellation requests, exact quote-bound change orders, safe published ETA and generic assigned-team state, issue escalation through the shared Staff Inbox thread, account approvals and rule administration, Quote V2, locations, proof, billing/documents, reports, help, messages, templates, recurring pause/resume/cancel, and CSV intake now exist; multi-stop routing, full provider reconciliation, and other release evidence remain partial.

The original data-integrity defects have also been addressed in architecture and focused tests: V2 rescheduling operates through a replacement hold and preserves the account-owned job, job/media ownership is explicit, and portal DTOs do not serialize raw appointment IDs or internal planned starts. These changes are not promoted to fully closed until their row-specific browser, concurrency, downstream reconciliation, provider, rehearsal, and production-smoke evidence exists.

The complete ledger contains **99 findings: 12 P0, 53 P1, 29 P2, and 5 P3**. Each finding includes the required improvement and its evidence.

The original audit records the required additions and corrections; the implementation completion ledger at the end is the live closure record. No row is silently waived, and no row is marked **Closed** without its required automated, manual, provider, operational, and rollout evidence.

## Required target capability checklist

This is a product-completeness checklist, not an implementation sequence.

### Acquire and onboard

- Separate **Sign in** and **Request/Create partner account** journeys.
- Verify person and organization; collect persona, service area, needs, terms/privacy consent, and approval status.
- Support invite/approval, first-login checklist, profile/company setup, colleague invitations, roles, and organization switching.
- Retain protected return URLs and provide clear recovery/support at every auth state.

### Schedule confidently

- Query one canonical availability engine using policy, holds, all booking channels, statuses, buffers, service duration, crews/trucks/skills, geography, blackout/holiday rules, and partner SLA.
- Display only valid slots with timezone, arrival-window meaning, price state, and constraint explanations.
- Hold the chosen slot while the partner reviews/submits; recover with alternatives if it expires.
- Preserve one transaction/locking contract across partner, public, and staff channels.

### Describe the work completely

- Capture property/unit, onsite contacts, access, scope/items/materials/quantities, hazards/heavy items, equipment, deadline, PO/cost center, billing reference, completion requirements, notes, and alternate options.
- Support service plus add-ons, contracted/estimated/quote-required pricing, recurring/templates/bulk jobs, and persona-aware fields.
- Provide an accessible review/terms/policy step and durable draft recovery.

### Use photos and proof

- Upload from camera/files with previews, captions/types, progress, retry, limits, and private tenant-scoped storage.
- Request before, after, or full proof; show staff requirements and completion state.
- Deliver a before/after gallery and downloadable/shareable proof report with issue handling and retention rules.

### Manage the job

- Provide a detail workspace with full submitted request, reference, status timeline, changes, contacts, media/proof, price/invoice/payment/documents, notifications, and support/messages.
- Permit safe, policy-aware edits/change requests, reschedule, cancel, duplicate/rebook, and dispute/escalation.
- Separate actionable upcoming work from searchable/paginated history and exports.

### Manage the account

- Provide properties/locations/units with validation, serviceability, aliases, contacts, sensitive access controls, edit/archive, search/filter/import.
- Provide profile, organization, users/roles/approvals, notifications, billing, security/sessions, contracts/rates, documents, support, and reporting.
- Keep canonical appointment, calendar/dispatch, pricing, revenue/payment, media, and audit data consistent.

## Verified strengths to preserve

- Real PostgreSQL persistence; property creation and booking workflows are transactional.
- Strong current organization scoping on property use, booking reads, cancellation, and rescheduling; no direct cross-organization IDOR was found in audited routes.

- Sessions are random, hashed, revocable, server-expiring, and revalidate an active canonical identity, eligible account, and active account membership on every request. CRM contacts are downstream operational projections, not authority.
- Production cookies are HTTP-only, Secure, and SameSite Lax.
- Admin deactivation revokes sessions and outstanding tokens transactionally.
- Booking creation/reschedule/cancel have meaningful idempotency, replay receipts, optimistic versioning, audit records, and transactional notification/calendar outbox work.

- Negotiated price is looked up server-side; callers cannot submit an arbitrary amount.
- Notes are bounded and reach internal appointment notes plus confirmation/staff messaging.
- Existing appointment media storage is private, purpose-aware, caption-capable, soft-deletable, and suitable as a foundation once partner authorization is designed.
- The current UI has coherent cards, labels, headings, visible focus styling, useful empty-state CTAs, no tested horizontal overflow, and pending protection on booking/cancel mutations.

## Baseline audit conclusion retained

The correct re-angle is not a visual reskin of the current five pages. The portal needs to become an account-aware scheduling and job workspace built on one canonical availability/conflict contract and one canonical job record. Live availability, protected photo/proof workflows, self-service onboarding, complete structured intake, and job detail/history are core architecture—not optional enhancements.

The existing authentication, tenancy checks, transactional booking mutations, idempotency, private media foundation, notifications, and basic responsive UI are worth preserving. The next planning pass should turn the P0/P1 findings into an implementation sequence with schema/API migration boundaries, UX flows, rollout/compatibility steps, and blocking acceptance tests.

The current disposition is governed by the completion ledger below: much of that re-angle now exists in the repository, while the remaining partial scope and release evidence keep the product from general availability.
