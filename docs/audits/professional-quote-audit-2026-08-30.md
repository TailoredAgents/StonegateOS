# Professional Quote creation and sender audit

Date: 2026-08-30  
Code reviewed: `main` at `b6c740d0`  
Primary surfaces: CRM **Quotes** tab, mobile Quotes screen, customer proposal, booking, sender, PDF, pricing, persistence, and tests  
Product readiness verdict: **Blocked for a professional commercial quote workflow**  
Implementation status: **Audit only. No product behavior was changed in this pass.**

## Executive verdict

The Quote experience has a clean visual base and unusually strong staff-side mutation primitives, but it is not yet safe or complete enough to be the authoritative professional-estimate workflow. It can create, issue, track, approve, and book a basic junk-removal quote, but critical customer actions and document-integrity guarantees break under normal revision, delivery, and error scenarios.

Five product release blockers were confirmed:

1. **“Request changes” silently does nothing.** The form omits fields required by its server action.
2. **A customer can accept or decline a revision they never saw.** Public decisions are not bound to the revision rendered in the browser.
3. **The line-item Total row and the no-availability approval button can be invisible.** They use an undefined `primary-950` color with white text.
4. **An availability outage is presented as a legitimately empty calendar.** The fallback invites approval while concealing that booking is unavailable, and current E2E branching can still pass when an availability failure removes the booking branch.
5. **A read-only employee receives the customer-action bearer token.** Removing `?preview=1` lets that user act through the public capability as though they were the customer.

The register contains **86 product/engineering findings**: 5 P0, 48 P1, 32 P2, and 1 P3. A separate section records **14 test/observability gaps**: 2 P0, 9 P1, and 3 P2. Related symptoms are kept separate when they require different fixes, so the next planning pass can close them without losing evidence.

The commercial-estimate gap is also substantial. The composer has no company/attention/PO/project fields, billing address, quantities, units, per-line descriptions, tax, explicit discounts, fees, alternates, attachments, configurable inclusions/exclusions, signature identity, or immutable accepted-document snapshot. The generated PDF is a technically valid but nearly unstyled text page, not a client-ready commercial proposal.

The strongest parts should be retained: canonical deep-linkable Quote routes; separate private/link/send choices; clean staff and customer card hierarchy; granular permissions; staff idempotency and optimistic concurrency; DNC and recipient validation; audit/outbox receipts; revision-bound hold-and-book operations; and a public page that is no-indexed, no-referrer, responsive, and free of horizontal overflow in the tested viewport.

## Scope and audit method

### Included

- `/team/quotes/create`: customer/property selection, services, custom pricing, duration, deposit terms, validity, AI scope drafting, draft/link/send choices, and post-submit behavior.
- `/team/quotes/manage`: search, filters, sorting, status model, delivery/view/PDF/change telemetry, send/resend, staff decisions, preview, and deletion.
- `/mobile?screen=quotes`: mobile creation, editing, sending, status actions, and parity with the canonical CRM.
- `/quote/[token]`: customer review, price/scope/terms, change requests, approval, decline, expiry refresh, availability, hold, booking, and responsive presentation.
- `/quote/[token]/pdf`: content, visual quality, state/access behavior, and tracking.
- Canonical staff/public APIs, pricing engine, quote schema, outbox delivery, notifications, pipeline effects, permissions, audit/idempotency, and scheduling.
- Existing unit, integration, E2E, visual-capture, and accessibility coverage.
- Instant Quotes only where it hands a lead into the professional Quote workflow or materially affects Quote-tab navigation/clutter. Its estimator model was not re-audited as a separate product.

### Runtime work performed

- Created a sanitized local client/property and built a `$1,250` Furniture Removal proposal through the real CRM form.
- Exercised private/customer-link behavior, staff Manage, Send, the public Draft and Sent states, delivery telemetry, the change-request form, and PDF download.
- Verified that both SMS and email were queued when both destinations existed.
- Verified that the customer-link Draft displayed “no longer open for online approval.”
- Submitted a public change request and confirmed that the database change-request count remained unchanged.
- Rendered desktop and 375 px customer pages. The sent quote produced **48 “Approve and book” buttons**, a **3,317 px desktop document**, and a **6,177 px mobile document**.
- Downloaded and visually inspected the one-page PDF.
- Ran axe-core WCAG 2.0/2.1 A/AA scans against staff Manage, staff Create at desktop/mobile, and the sent public page at desktop/mobile.
- Reviewed source, schema, API boundaries, outbox handlers, pricing, notification copy, and test coverage end to end.

## Current workflow map

```text
CRM Quotes / Create
  -> choose customer/property
  -> choose one or more hard-coded services
  -> manually enter one total per service
  -> optional AI scope
  -> create private draft OR bearer link OR create-and-send
  -> flash message; remain on the form

CRM Quotes / Manage
  -> filter/search all quotes loaded into the browser
  -> send/resend | mark approve/reject | public-token preview | limited delete

Customer bearer link
  -> review total/scope/terms
  -> request changes | decline | approve without a slot | approve-and-book a slot
  -> optional PDF download
```

The missing durable concept is a **published quote version**. Today, one mutable `quotes` row is simultaneously the draft, sent proposal, viewed document, PDF source, accepted document, and booking source.

## Severity definitions

- **P0 — Release blocker:** can cause unauthorized action, false customer success, acceptance of unseen terms, hidden conversion controls, or a core journey to silently fail.
- **P1 — Must fix for the stated goal:** material correctness, professionalism, reliability, scale, or task-completion gap.
- **P2 — Important improvement:** meaningful friction, ambiguity, accessibility, resilience, or maintainability issue.
- **P3 — Polish/operational improvement:** lower-risk clarity or quality issue.

## Strengths to preserve

1. **Clear access points.** Create, Manage, and Instant are canonical, directly linkable routes, and only the active workspace is loaded.
2. **Honest permission-aware server states.** Quote read/write/send/update/delete are distinct; unavailable/read-only states exist.
3. **Strong staff mutation safety.** Create/update/send/decision/delete use human principals, explicit confirmation contracts, idempotency, and audit records. Update/send/decision/delete add row locking, expected versions, and compare-and-swap where applicable.
4. **Recipient safety.** Send rechecks an active contact under lock, enforces DNC, and validates normalized email/phone destinations.
5. **Transactional delivery initiation.** Quote state, outbox event, audit, and idempotency receipt commit together; channel messages have durable dedupe keys and dispatch/reconciliation records.
6. **Safe token handling in audit receipts.** Strong `nanoid(24)` tokens are unique; raw bearer links are intentionally scrubbed from mutation receipts/audit and placeholdered before AI generation.
7. **Contact/property integrity on creation.** The canonical create path validates the relationship transactionally; Instant handoff verifies and locks the intended selection.
8. **Booking primitives.** Hold/book uses strict bounded requests, revision binding, advisory schedule-conflict locks, business-hours/capacity checks, quote/contact/property binding, idempotent receipts, and transactional appointment/pipeline/audit work.
9. **Terminal edit protection.** Accepted/declined quotes cannot be edited through the canonical PATCH route.
10. **Useful CRM telemetry foundation.** Manage already exposes search, filters, derived status, delivery, view, PDF, expiry, and separate desktop/mobile presentations.
11. **Good public-page baseline.** Clear price/scope hierarchy, generous spacing, support links, terms, PDF access, sticky desktop action column, no-index/no-referrer metadata, and no tested horizontal overflow.
12. **Explicit creation choices.** Private draft, customer link, and provider delivery are modeled separately and never silently selected when a client changes.

## Capabilities that must be added or re-angled

This is the minimum target capability set implied by the audit, not an implementation plan.

### 1. Versioned professional proposal model

- Immutable draft/published/sent/accepted versions.
- Full parties, company, project/site, reference/PO, owner/preparer, line items, scope, assumptions, exclusions, terms, expiry, attachments, and totals on the version.
- Explicit states: Draft → Ready/Published → Sent → Viewed → Changes requested → Revised/Superseded → Accepted → Booked, with Declined/Expired/Voided branches.
- Every view, download, change, decision, send attempt, and appointment references a version.

### 2. Focused staff composer

- Step 1: client/company, recipient, billing/service property, project/reference.
- Step 2: reusable or custom line items with quantity/unit/rate, discounts/fees/tax, scope, exclusions, photos/files, and alternates.
- Step 3: duration, validity, payment/deposit schedule, availability mode, terms.
- Step 4: exact web/PDF/message preview, recipients/channels, and Review & Send.
- Sticky authoritative totals, autosave, draft recovery, and inline client/property creation.

### 3. Quote detail and revision workspace

- Edit draft, publish, preview, copy/revoke/rotate link, send/resend selected channel, duplicate, revise, supersede, void/archive.
- Full activity/version timeline and diff.
- Change-request queue/message, decision evidence, delivery attempts, appointment, owner, next action, reminders, and audit context.

### 4. Conversion-first customer proposal

- Company/project-aware hero with total, validity, version, and primary CTA immediately visible.
- Concise expandable scope, inclusions/exclusions, pricing, terms, and attachments.
- Sticky mobile Approve / Request changes controls; clear decline nearby.
- Compact timezone-aware schedule picker with recommended slots and review confirmation.
- State-specific and error-specific UI; no CRM tracking labels.

### 5. Professional sender and document package

- Recipient/channel/preference/consent controls, commercial attention/billing recipients, CC/BCC, and exact cover-message preview.
- Deterministic critical facts, optional constrained tone assistance, localized dates, branded HTML/text, and immutable PDF attachment/link.
- Per-channel status, failure reason, retry, and reconciliation.
- Branded multipage PDF with company/legal contact, project/parties, detailed table, totals, terms/version, footer, and acceptance/signature evidence.

### 6. Safe customer response and scheduling

- Revision-bound accept, decline, changes, refresh, hold, and book.
- Accepted-by identity/authority and explicit consent tied to immutable terms/document hash.
- One active hold per quote, rate limiting, conflict-safe booking, duplicate-job invariant, and one combined accepted/booked notification.
- Opportunity-scoped monotonic pipeline and follow-up transitions.

### 7. Accessibility, consistency, and measured scale

- Shared semantic light/dark tokens and shared desktop/mobile domain behavior.
- WCAG 2.2 AA semantics, contrast, labels/errors/live regions, keyboard/focus, zoom, touch targets, and responsive baselines.
- Paginated/searchable APIs and measured 1k/10k query/response budgets.
- Deterministic E2E gates for every status, delivery mode, revision conflict, booking branch, and failure state.

## Release gates before calling the Quote tab professional

1. QTE-001 through QTE-006 are fixed and covered by deterministic browser tests.
2. Published/sent/accepted content is immutable and every customer mutation is version-bound.
3. Pricing invariants reconcile across composer, API, message, web, PDF, deposit, and booking.
4. Change requests create exactly one durable staff-visible action and pause the appropriate opportunity follow-up.
5. Customer links can be safely created, copied, rotated, revoked, expired, and previewed without leaking mutation authority to read-only staff.
6. The staff user can review totals/content, send safely, recover a failed send, open detail, and revise without recreating the quote.
7. The mobile customer can reach a clear CTA quickly, distinguish errors from no capacity, choose a timezone-labelled window without 48-button overload, and confirm before booking.
8. The PDF and email meet the commercial document checklist and exactly represent an immutable version.
9. Serious/critical automated accessibility findings are zero or explicitly justified, and keyboard/mobile/zoom journeys pass.
10. Quote list/search and public performance meet defined budgets at realistic volume.

## Bottom line

The existing Quote tab is a promising basic quote generator with good staff-side safety primitives and a pleasant visual skeleton. It is not yet a dependable professional estimate system because it lacks immutable proposal versions, robust customer-response handling, scalable staff workflows, commercial document depth, and a conversion-focused booking experience. The next planning pass should protect document/customer integrity first, then consolidate the composer/detail/sender model, and only then polish the public booking and visual package around that stable foundation.
