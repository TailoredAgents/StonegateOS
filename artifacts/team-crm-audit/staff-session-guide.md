# `/team` staff validation guide

This guide is ready for the five required 45-minute sessions. No staff sessions were performed during the repository-only audit.

## Participants

- One owner.
- Two office/sales users with different tenure.
- Two crew users.

Use a sanitized staging clone. Do not use production customer records or live provider credentials.

## Session structure

1. Five-minute introduction: ask the participant to think aloud; explain that the product, not the person, is being tested.
2. Thirty minutes of role-relevant tasks without coaching.
3. Five minutes of targeted follow-up on confusing or unused features.
4. Five minutes of prioritization and a 1–7 ease score per task.

## Owner tasks

- Find why yesterday's revenue and collected payments disagree.
- Reconcile one unmatched payment, then find its audit evidence.
- Review a draft payout, add a reimbursement, lock it, and explain what is now immutable.
- Create a custom role that can read quotes but cannot delete them; verify it using a second session.
- Change one quiet-hours rule, prove it in Simulated Chat, and restore the fixture.

## Office/sales tasks

- Respond to a new inbound lead, complete missing contact/property details, create/send a quote, and book the accepted job.
- Recover a stalled lead from Sales HQ and schedule the next follow-up.
- Import a small outbound CSV, assign records, log a disposition/callback, and locate the resulting history.
- Find a specific older conversation using Inbox filters and pagination.

## Crew tasks

- Find today's assigned job, open directions/context, add a note/photo, and update ETA.
- Complete the job with a final total and identify what the customer owes.
- Record a permitted expense with a receipt and verify what can/cannot be corrected.

## Measurements

For each task record:

- First click and whether it was correct.
- Completion and duration.
- Errors, backtracks, dead ends, and assistance.
- Data-integrity result.
- Post-task ease score from 1 (very difficult) to 7 (very easy).
- Direct quotes only with participant consent; otherwise paraphrase.

## Closing questions

- What should be kept as-is?
- What should be simplified?
- What belongs together?
- What should move?
- What belongs under Advanced or Diagnostics?
- What would you remove?
- What do you avoid because you do not trust or understand it?

Record unsupported utility conclusions as hypotheses until the relevant role completes a session.
